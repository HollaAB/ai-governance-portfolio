# Project Charter: Integrated GRC and Security Management System

> **Portfolio project.** This is an independent learning project using a fictional organisation and illustrative data. It is not an audit, legal opinion, or certification.

Draft 0.1, prepared for review by Abel Holla.

## Purpose

The five earlier projects each looked at one function. A real incident does not respect those lines: the "Saturday Medal" phishing attack in the [Stage 5 tabletop exercise](../../security-risk-incident-management/06-tabletop-exercise/scenario.md) involved weak access control (security), breach notification and processor duties (compliance), and unclear authority to warn members (governance). This capstone joins the five projects into one evidence trail around that incident and gives the Management Committee of Kijani Heights Golf and Country Club a single view and a single remediation plan.

## Scope

**Included:** an integrated case study and evidence trail; a requirements-to-controls mapping that follows each key requirement to its control, test, finding and action; an integrated risk and control register; cross-cutting findings; one remediation roadmap built from every project's open actions; a portfolio dashboard and committee paper; an executive report, presentation outline and lessons learned.

**Excluded:** new testing beyond the earlier projects; legal advice.

## Intended outcomes

- One picture of the Club's compliance, governance, data, AI and security position.
- Every key requirement traceable from law or policy to evidence and action.
- One prioritised roadmap the Committee can approve and track.
