# Enterprise Governance and Risk Dashboard

> **Portfolio project.** This is an independent learning project using a fictional organisation and illustrative data. It is not an audit, legal opinion, or certification.

**Status:** In progress (Stage 3 draft for review) | **Stage:** 3 | **Area:** Governance

**Organisation:** Kijani Heights Golf and Country Club (fictional), see the [organisation profile](../scenario/organisation-profile.md)

**Notion page:** [TODO: link, added after publishing]

## 1. Overview and business problem

At Kijani Heights Golf and Country Club, high-risk technology went live without Committee review, decisions were minuted but not followed up, and there was no agreed risk appetite. The [Stage 2 compliance review](../compliance-management-system/07-reporting/management-report.md) traced several findings back to these governance gaps.

## 2. Objectives, scope and assumptions

Set out who decides what, oversee risk against an agreed appetite, and track every Committee decision to closure, then report compliance, risk and governance on one quarterly dashboard. Changes to the Articles and legal advice are out of scope. Assumptions: [assumptions register](01-project-charter/assumptions-register.csv).

## 3. Frameworks and methodology

ISO 37000:2021 governance principles for the maturity assessment; ISO 31000:2018 concepts for risk appetite and escalation. No certification is claimed. Details: [methodology](02-methodology/methodology.md).

## 5. Testing results or evaluation criteria

Governance was scored against the eleven ISO 37000 principles ([maturity assessment](07-improvement/governance-maturity-assessment.csv)): baseline 2.5 out of 5, with risk governance lowest. 9 of 18 risks were outside appetite. After one quarter (scenario results): maturity 3.0, four of eight governance findings closed, and two of four High compliance findings closed through Committee decisions.

## 6. Recommendations and implementation roadmap

Collect conflict of interest declarations (January 2027), obtain the caddie legal opinion (January 2027), co-opt a member with technology skills, and keep the [dashboard](08-reporting/dashboard-data.csv) as a standing quarterly item. Actions: [governance corrective-action tracker](07-improvement/corrective-action-tracker.csv). See the [committee report](08-reporting/committee-report.md).

## 7. Limitations and lessons learned

See [limitations](limitations.md). Lessons: the Stage 2 data protection gaps had a governance cause (no rule on who approves high-risk technology), and governance becomes visible only when decisions are tracked through to closure evidence.
