# Project Charter: AI Governance Framework

> **Portfolio project.** This is an independent learning project using a fictional organisation and illustrative data. It is not an audit, legal opinion, or certification.

Draft 0.1, prepared for review by Abel Holla.

## Purpose

Kijani Heights Golf and Country Club launched an AI chatbot and number-plate recognition without any AI governance. The Management Committee wants a framework that lists every AI system, classifies its risk, assesses and tests high-risk systems before use, keeps a human in control, checks AI vendors, and records incidents and changes.

## Scope

**Included:** AI governance charter and lifecycle; AI system inventory; risk classification method and results; impact assessment and testing of Kijani Assist; human oversight matrix; staff acceptable-use policy for generative AI; vendor AI due diligence; model change and AI incident registers; findings, corrective actions and reporting.

**Excluded:** personal data assessments (in the [DPIAs](../../data-protection-privacy-assurance/04-dpia/)), and building or training AI models.

## Intended outcomes

- No AI system is used without being listed, classified and, if high-risk, assessed, tested and approved.
- Every AI system has a named owner and a human who can override it.
- Vendors are checked before and during use; changes and incidents are recorded.
