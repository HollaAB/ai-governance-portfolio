**Kijani Heights Golf and Country Club** ("the Club") is the data controller for the personal data described here. Our Data Protection Officer can be reached through reception or the member app.

## What we collect and why

| What | Why | Lawful basis |
|---|---|---|
| Your membership details, ID and photo | To manage your membership | Contract |
| Your account and payment history | To bill you and take payments | Contract |
| Bookings, handicap and competition results | To run bookings and competitions | Contract |
| Your messages to Kijani Assist | To answer your questions and make bookings | Contract |
| Your contact details for promotions | To tell you about events, if you agree | Consent |
| CCTV images | To keep people and property safe | Legitimate interests |
| Your vehicle registration at the gate | To open the gate automatically, if you opt in to ANPR | Consent |
| Visitor details at the gate | To control access | Legitimate interests |

## Kijani Assist

Kijani Assist is an automated assistant. It sends the text of your questions to a language model provider outside Kenya to draft answers; your account details are not sent. Please do not share health or other sensitive information in the chat.

## Who we share data with

Our processors (club management system, chatbot, payroll and ICT providers, guarding company, events partner), under written agreements; the national golf union for handicaps; payment providers; and authorities where the law requires.

## Transfers outside Kenya

Member records are hosted in South Africa, and chatbot questions are processed outside Kenya, under agreements that protect your data.

## How long we keep it

See our retention schedule; for example, CCTV and ANPR records are kept for 30 days unless needed for an incident.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, object to its use, or withdraw consent. Ask at reception, in the app, or by message to Kijani Assist ("data request"). We aim to respond within 7 days. You can also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

## CCTV and ANPR sign text (for all 8 entry points)

**CCTV and number plate recognition in use.** Kijani Heights Golf and Country Club records images and, at the main gate, reads number plates of members who have opted in, to keep people and property safe and to speed up entry. Recordings are kept for 30 days. Ask at reception or see our privacy notice for details and your rights.
