## When a DPIA is needed

Before any new or changed processing that involves one or more of:

- systematic monitoring of people (CCTV, ANPR, location tracking);
- sensitive personal data (health, biometrics, children's data) on a regular basis;
- automated decisions with a significant effect on people;
- new technology, including AI;
- large volumes of personal data or data sent outside Kenya.

The project owner completes a screening form; the Compliance Officer (DPO) decides whether a full DPIA is needed.

## Steps

1. Describe the processing and its purpose.
2. Assess necessity and proportionality, including the lawful basis.
3. Identify risks to individuals and score them (likelihood x impact, 1 to 25).
4. Agree measures with the owner and re-score the risks.
5. Record the DPO's opinion.
6. Approve: General Manager for low or medium residual risk; Management Committee for high-risk processing ([delegation of authority](https://github.com/HollaAB/ai-governance-portfolio/blob/main/enterprise-governance-risk-dashboard/03-governance-framework/delegation-of-authority.csv)). Consult the ODPC if a high residual risk remains.
7. Review the DPIA after six months and whenever the processing changes.
