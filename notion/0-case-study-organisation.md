> **Fictional organisation.** Kijani Heights Golf and Country Club is invented for this portfolio. It is modelled on member golf clubs in Kenya but does not describe any real club. All names, figures and systems are illustrative.

## Profile

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Name | Kijani Heights Golf and Country Club ("the Club") |
| Type | Private members' golf club |
| Legal form | Company limited by guarantee, governed by its Articles and Club Bylaws |
| Location | Kiambu County, about 25 km from Nairobi |
| Members | About 1,850 (full, corporate, country, junior and honorary), plus dependants |
| Employees | About 165 permanent and contract staff |
| Caddies | About 140, registered with the Club and paid per round (not employees) |
| Contracted guards | 36, supplied by a private security company |
| Facilities | 18-hole course, 9-hole academy course, driving range, clubhouse with restaurant, two bars and a banqueting hall, gym and pool, pro shop (concession) |

## Governance

- **Members in General Meeting:** elect the Management Committee and approve the annual accounts at the AGM.
- **Management Committee (board):** nine elected members, including the Chairman, Vice Chairman, Captain and Honorary Treasurer.
- **Sub-committees:** Finance and Audit; Course and Greens; Membership; House and Catering; Security and Safety.
- **General Manager:** runs daily operations and reports to the Management Committee.
- **Management team:** Finance Manager, HR Manager, Course Manager, Food and Beverage Manager, Head of Security, ICT Officer, Membership and Events Manager, Compliance Officer (also the designated Data Protection Officer).

## Key systems

| System | Use | Hosting |
|---|---|---|
| Club management system ("FairwayOne", fictional vendor) | Membership records, billing, tee-time booking, member mobile app | Cloud, hosted in South Africa |
| Point of sale | Restaurant, bars and events | On site, linked to member accounts |
| Payments | M-Pesa paybill and card terminals through the Club's bank | Third-party providers |
| Payroll | Run by an outsourced payroll bureau | Bureau's cloud system |
| CCTV and gate access | 64 cameras, boom gates, visitor register | On site; automatic number plate recognition (ANPR) under pilot |
| Email and documents | Cloud email and office suite | Cloud |

## AI-assisted member service

**Kijani Assist** (fictional) is a chatbot on the member app and WhatsApp, built by a local vendor on a third-party large language model. It answers questions about Club rules and events, books tee times, and, after identity checks, shares account balances. The Club is also piloting ANPR at the main gate to admit members' registered vehicles automatically.

## Personal data held

- **Members and dependants:** names, ID or passport numbers, phone numbers, email, photos, vehicle registrations, handicap records, account and payment history.
- **Guests and visitors:** names, ID numbers, vehicle registrations, CCTV images.
- **Employees:** HR files, payroll and bank details, KRA PIN, NSSF and SHIF numbers, medical certificates for food handlers (health data).
- **Caddies:** names, ID numbers, phone and M-Pesa numbers, round records.
- **Job applicants:** CVs and interview notes.

## Starting position

Compliance is handled informally by the General Manager and the Compliance Officer. There is no single list of the Club's obligations, renewals are tracked in personal calendars, and evidence is spread across departments. After public enforcement action by the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner against other organisations, the Management Committee asked for a compliance management system. That request is the first project in this portfolio.
