All results, dates and decisions are scenario results for the fictional Club.

## 1. Description

64 cameras cover the gates, car parks, clubhouse entrances, bars, the pro shop and the course maintenance yard. Footage goes to an on-site recorder; Linda Guard Services views live feeds. Since August 2026 a camera at the main gate has read number plates and opened the barrier for members' registered vehicles. Expansion to the staff gate was planned.

## 2. Necessity and proportionality

| Question | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Is there a clear purpose? | Yes: safety of people and property, and faster entry for members. |
| Could less intrusive means work? | For CCTV, no: guards cannot cover all areas. For ANPR, a members' sticker or card would work, so ANPR must be optional. |
| Is data limited to what is needed? | No cameras in changing rooms or toilets. ANPR stores plate and time only. |
| How long is data kept? | Previously 180 days for CCTV and indefinitely for ANPR; reduced to 30 days (see measures). |
| Lawful basis | CCTV: legitimate interests, balanced against members' expectations. ANPR: consent of members who opt in; non-members' plates are not matched. |
| Automated decisions | ANPR opens the gate automatically. A guard handles any non-match, so no one is refused entry by the system alone (Data Protection Act 2019, s.35). |

## 3. Risks

| ID | Risk to individuals | Likelihood (1 to 5) | Impact (1 to 5) | Score before measures |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DR-01 | Footage of members kept far longer than needed | 5 | 3 | 15 |
| DR-02 | Guards or staff misuse footage (for example sharing clips) | 3 | 4 | 12 |
| DR-03 | ANPR logs reveal members' movement patterns | 4 | 3 | 12 |
| DR-04 | A misread plate admits a non-member or refuses a member | 3 | 2 | 6 |
| DR-05 | People are not told about CCTV or ANPR | 4 | 2 | 8 |

## 4. Measures

| Risk | Measure | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| DR-01 | Retention set to 30 days for CCTV and ANPR, unless footage is kept for an incident (retention schedule RS-06, RS-07) | Head of Security |
| DR-02 | Access to recordings limited to the Head of Security and two named guards; every viewing and export logged; data processing agreement with Linda Guard Services | Head of Security |
| DR-03 | ANPR only for members who opt in; no reports on individual movements without a written incident request | Head of Security |
| DR-04 | Guard procedure for non-matches; monthly check of misread rate | Head of Security |
| DR-05 | CCTV and ANPR signs at all 8 entry points; privacy notice updated | Compliance Officer (DPO) |

| ID | Score after measures | Rating |

## 6. DPO opinion

The processing can continue with the measures above. ANPR must remain opt-in, and expansion to the staff gate needs a separate assessment because staff cannot freely refuse. Prior consultation with the ODPC is not needed because no residual risk is high.

## 7. Decision

The Management Committee approved ANPR as an opt-in service for members at the main gate, with the measures above, on 9 December 2026 (D-2026-12-01). Expansion to the staff gate is not approved.
