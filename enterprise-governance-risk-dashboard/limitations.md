# Limitations: Enterprise Governance and Risk Dashboard

> **Portfolio project.** This is an independent learning project using a fictional organisation and illustrative data. It is not an audit, legal opinion, or certification.

- **Fictional organisation.** The Club, its Committee, meetings, decisions and figures are invented. Board papers and minutes are examples of format and content, not records of any real meeting.
- **Scenario results.** Maturity scores, dashboard values and closure dates are scenario results chosen to show how the oversight cycle works.
- **Framework use.** The maturity assessment uses the ISO 37000:2021 principle names as a structure. It is not a formal assessment against the standard, and no certification is implied.
- **Thresholds.** Financial limits in the delegation of authority and KRI thresholds are illustrative; a real club would set them from its own budget and history.
- **Legal points.** The caddie engagement question needs a real legal opinion; this project only records it as a risk and a decision.
