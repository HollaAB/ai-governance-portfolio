# Methodology: Enterprise Governance and Risk Dashboard

Prepared by Abel Holla.

1. **Baseline.** Review the Club's Articles, Bylaws, committee minutes for the last 12 months and the Stage 2 findings. Score current practice against the eleven ISO 37000:2021 governance principles on a 1 to 5 scale in the [maturity assessment](../07-improvement/governance-maturity-assessment.csv).
2. **Design the framework.** Set out the governance structure, reserved matters and delegation of authority, and refresh the terms of reference of the two sub-committees that oversee risk and safety.
3. **Assign accountability.** Map key processes to roles in a RACI matrix, and set an escalation matrix that says what goes to whom and how fast.
4. **Govern risk.** Write a risk appetite statement by risk category, compare every risk in the enterprise risk register with it, and define key risk indicators (KRIs) with green, amber and red thresholds.
5. **Show oversight in action.** Take the Stage 2 compliance results through a full cycle: board paper, committee decision, recorded actions with owners and deadlines, follow-up, and closure evidence.
6. **Measure.** Report governance KPIs and KRIs on a quarterly dashboard and in a committee report.
7. **Improve.** Record each maturity gap as a finding with a corrective action.

## Maturity scale

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | Not in place |
| 2 | Informal, depends on individuals |
| 3 | Defined and documented |
| 4 | Operating consistently, with evidence |
| 5 | Monitored and improved |

## Reference frameworks

ISO 37000:2021 (governance of organisations) for the principles; ISO 31000:2018 (risk management) for risk appetite and escalation concepts. Using these frameworks does not mean the Club or this project is certified against them.
