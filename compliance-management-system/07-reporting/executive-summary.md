# Executive Summary: Compliance Management System

> **Portfolio project.** This is an independent learning project using a fictional organisation and illustrative data. It is not an audit, legal opinion, or certification.

All results are scenario results for the fictional Club.

**The problem.** Kijani Heights Golf and Country Club, a fictional members' club in Kiambu County, had no single view of its obligations. Renewals sat in personal calendars and evidence was scattered.

**What was done.** 37 obligations were identified across data protection, anti-bribery, employment, health and safety, tax, licensing, environment, corporate and contractual duties; 36 apply. Each was given an owner, a control and an evidence item, and a compliance calendar was set. All 22 controls were then tested in a gap assessment.

**What was found.** 18 obligations (50%) are fully met; 14 are partly met and 4 are not met. 7 of 22 controls work as intended. Of 16 findings, 4 are High: missing DPIAs for the ANPR pilot and AI chatbot, processor contracts without data protection terms, no breach procedure, and an overdue fire drill.

**What is recommended.** Fix the four High findings within 30 days, approve the compliance policy, and report the seven compliance KPIs to the Finance and Audit sub-committee every quarter.

**What this demonstrates.** Turning legal duties into owned, testable controls; evidence-based testing with sampling; graded findings with corrective actions; and reporting that a board can act on.
