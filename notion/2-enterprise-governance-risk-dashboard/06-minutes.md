## 1. Declarations of interest

## 2. Compliance gap assessment (paper MC-2026-10-03)

The Compliance Officer presented the results. Members asked why the ANPR pilot started without review. The General Manager explained that no rule required Committee approval for technology projects within budget. The Committee agreed this was a gap in authority, not an individual failing.

Members discussed whether to stop the ANPR pilot entirely. The Committee agreed to keep the existing gate camera running for security but to pause expansion until the DPIA is complete.
