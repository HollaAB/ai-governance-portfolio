> **Portfolio project.** This is an independent learning project using a fictional organisation and illustrative data. It is not an audit, legal opinion, or certification.

Draft 0.1, prepared for review by Abel Holla.

## Purpose

To make sure the Club learns about new or changed laws, regulations, county rules and contract terms in time, and updates its obligations register, controls and calendar.

## Owner

Compliance Officer (DPO), reporting to the General Manager. The Finance and Audit sub-committee oversees the procedure.

## Sources monitored

| Source | What to watch | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Kenya Gazette and Kenya Law | New acts, amendments, regulations and legal notices | Monthly |
| Office of the Data Protection Commissioner | Guidance notes, determinations, enforcement notices | Monthly |
| Kenya Revenue Authority | Tax rates, filing changes, public notices | Monthly |
| Kiambu County Government | Finance Act, permit fees, liquor and public health rules | Quarterly and at each county budget cycle |
| Directorate of Occupational Safety and Health | Rules and inspection notices | Quarterly |
| Water Resources Authority and NEMA | Permit conditions, audit requirements | Quarterly |
| Bank, payment and supplier contracts | Changed terms and security requirements | At renewal |
| National golf union | Affiliation and handicap rule changes | Annually |
| Professional bodies and the Club's lawyers | Alerts and briefings | As received |

## Steps

1. **Capture.** Log each change found in the regulatory change log: date, source, summary and effective date.
2. **Assess.** Within 10 working days, decide whether it applies to the Club and which obligations, controls and owners it affects.
3. **Update.** Add or amend entries in the obligations register, the obligations-to-controls matrix and the compliance calendar. Increase the register version and record the change in the version history.
4. **Assign.** Give each required action an owner and a deadline in the corrective-action tracker.
5. **Communicate.** Tell affected owners in writing, and include material changes in the next management report.
6. **Report.** Summarise changes and open actions to the Finance and Audit sub-committee each quarter.
7. **Review.** Once a year, review the whole register for applicability (calendar entry CAL-20).

## Records

- Regulatory change log (date, source, change, applies, affected obligations, action, owner, status).
- Version history of the obligations register.
- Quarterly reports to the Finance and Audit sub-committee.

## Escalation

A change that takes effect in less than 30 days, or that creates a risk of penalties, is escalated to the General Manager the same day.
