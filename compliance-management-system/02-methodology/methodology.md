# Methodology: Compliance Management System

Draft 0.1, prepared for review by Abel Holla.

## Stage 1: Obligations register

1. **Set the scope.** Use the [organisation profile](../../scenario/organisation-profile.md) to list the Club's activities: membership, food and beverage, events, course maintenance, employment, payments, security, technology and AI services.
2. **Identify sources.** For each activity, list the laws, regulations, county laws, contracts and internal rules that could apply. Starting points: the Data Protection Act 2019 and its regulations, Office of the Data Protection Commissioner guidance, the Bribery Act 2016, employment, safety, tax, licensing and environmental law, and the Club's own Articles and Bylaws.
3. **Assess applicability.** For each source, record whether it applies to the Club and why, based on the Club's legal form, size, location and activities. Sources that do not apply are kept in the register with the reason, so the decision is visible.
4. **Assign owners.** Give each applicable obligation an accountable role from the management team.
5. **Map to controls.** Define a proposed control for each obligation and record it in the obligations-to-controls matrix. One control may cover several obligations.
6. **Set the calendar.** Turn every recurring or event-driven duty into a calendar entry with an owner, deadline rule and reminder.
7. **Define evidence.** For each obligation, state what record proves it is met and where it is kept.
8. **Check traceability.** Every applicable obligation must link to an owner, a control and an evidence item (see the validation rules in the [data dictionary](../03-obligations-register/data-dictionary.md)).
9. **Verify sources.** Each legal reference was checked against the official texts by Abel Holla (October 2026) and marked "Verified".

## Stage 2: Gap assessment (to follow)

Each proposed control will be tested against its evidence. Results will be recorded as findings with a severity, and each finding will get a corrective action with an owner and a due date.

## Reference framework

The approach follows the structure of ISO 37301:2021 (compliance management systems): identify obligations, assess compliance risk, put controls in place, monitor and improve. Using the framework does not mean the Club or this project is certified against it.
