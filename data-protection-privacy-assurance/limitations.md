# Limitations: Data Protection and Privacy Assurance

> **Portfolio project.** This is an independent learning project using a fictional organisation and illustrative data. It is not an audit, legal opinion, or certification.

- **Fictional organisation.** The Club, its suppliers (including ModelCo), requests and the breach are invented.
- **Scenario results.** Counts, dates and residual risk scores are scenario results.
- **Legal points to verify.** Section numbers, the statutory request period, retention minimums and any ODPC notification for transfers must be checked against the official texts. Legal references are marked "to verify" where the exact section or period has not been checked against the official text.
- **Not legal advice.** The DPIAs show method and reasoning; a real club would take legal advice on ANPR consent and on the balancing test for CCTV.
- **Not an audit.** The assurance review is an internal review of the Club's own controls.
