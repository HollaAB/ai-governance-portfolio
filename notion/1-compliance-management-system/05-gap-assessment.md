> **Portfolio project.** This is an independent learning project using a fictional organisation and illustrative data. It is not an audit, legal opinion, or certification.

Draft 0.2, prepared for review by Abel Holla.

## Scope

All 36 applicable obligations in the [obligations register](https://github.com/HollaAB/ai-governance-portfolio/blob/main/compliance-management-system/03-obligations-register/compliance-register.csv) and the 22 controls in the [controls matrix](https://github.com/HollaAB/ai-governance-portfolio/blob/main/compliance-management-system/03-obligations-register/obligations-to-controls-matrix.csv). Fieldwork: 12 to 23 October 2026 (scenario dates).

## Approach

1. **Prepare.** For each obligation, write the requirement and the test step in the [requirements checklist](https://github.com/HollaAB/ai-governance-portfolio/blob/main/compliance-management-system/04-gap-assessment/requirements-checklist.csv). Send the [evidence request list](https://github.com/HollaAB/ai-governance-portfolio/blob/main/compliance-management-system/04-gap-assessment/evidence-request-list.csv) to owners five working days before fieldwork.
2. **Interview.** Meet each owner using the [interview questionnaire](https://github.com/HollaAB/ai-governance-portfolio/blob/main/compliance-management-system/04-gap-assessment/interview-questionnaire.md).
3. **Test.** For each control, inspect the evidence, take a sample where there is a population (files, messages, certificates, months of filings), and record the result in the [testing workpapers](https://github.com/HollaAB/ai-governance-portfolio/blob/main/compliance-management-system/04-gap-assessment/testing-workpapers.csv).
4. **Rate.** Give each control a result and each obligation a status (scales below).
5. **Report.** Record each exception as a finding in the [findings register](https://github.com/HollaAB/ai-governance-portfolio/blob/main/compliance-management-system/06-remediation/findings-register.csv), agree a corrective action with the owner, and summarise in the [management report](https://github.com/HollaAB/ai-governance-portfolio/blob/main/compliance-management-system/07-reporting/management-report.md).
6. **Follow up.** Retest each action after its due date and record closure in the [corrective-action tracker](https://github.com/HollaAB/ai-governance-portfolio/blob/main/compliance-management-system/06-remediation/corrective-action-tracker.csv).

## Sampling

| Population size | Sample |
|---|---|
| 1 to 5 items | All items |
| 6 to 50 | At least 10, or all if fewer |
| More than 50 | 25 items, selected at random |

## Control results

| Result | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Tested and effective | Evidence shows the control works for the whole sample |
| Tested with exceptions | The control exists but failed for part of the sample |
| Not implemented | The control does not exist |

## Obligation status

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Compliant | All controls for the obligation are tested and effective |
| Partly compliant | At least one control has exceptions, but the obligation is mostly met |
| Not compliant | The obligation is not met, or its control is not implemented |
| Not applicable | The obligation does not apply to the Club |

## Finding severity

| Severity | Meaning | Action due |
|---|---|---|
| High | Likely penalty, harm to people, or loss of a licence | Within 30 days |
| Medium | Partial breach or weak control with limited immediate impact | Within 90 days |
| Low | Documentation or process gap with little direct impact | Within 180 days |

## Risk scoring (used in the risk and control register)

Score = likelihood (1 to 5) x impact (1 to 5).

| Score | Rating |
|---|---|
| 1 to 4 | Low |
| 5 to 9 | Medium |
| 10 to 16 | High |
| 20 to 25 | Critical |

# Interview Questionnaire

Used with each obligation owner during fieldwork. Notes are kept in the testing workpapers.

## For every owner

1. Which obligations in the register are you responsible for? Do you agree with that list?
2. How do you make sure each one is met? Who does the work day to day?
3. What records show it was done, and where are they kept?
4. When was it last done? Was it on time?
5. Has anything gone wrong in the last 12 months (late filing, complaint, inspection finding, penalty)?
6. What would stop you meeting the obligation (staff, budget, systems, suppliers)?
7. How would you find out if the law or the contract changed?

## Data protection (Compliance Officer, ICT Officer, Membership and Events Manager)

8. Which systems hold personal data, and who can access them?
9. Which suppliers process personal data for the Club? Do contracts cover data protection?
10. What happens if a laptop is lost or a system is hacked? Who is told, and when?
11. How are requests from members to see or delete their data handled?
12. Was any assessment done before the ANPR pilot or the Kijani Assist chatbot started?
13. How long are CCTV footage and former members' records kept?

## Anti-bribery (General Manager, Finance Manager)

14. How are gifts and hospitality from suppliers recorded?
15. How can staff or members report a concern in confidence?

## Health, safety and licensing (HR Manager, Head of Security, F&B Manager, Course Manager)

16. When were the last fire drill and fire audit?
17. How are licence and certificate expiry dates tracked?
18. How are food handlers' medical certificates kept current?
19. Which pesticides are used, and how is their registration checked?
