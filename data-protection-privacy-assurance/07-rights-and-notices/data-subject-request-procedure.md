# Data Subject Request Procedure

> **Portfolio project.** This is an independent learning project using a fictional organisation and illustrative data. It is not an audit, legal opinion, or certification.

Prepared by Abel Holla.

## Scope

Requests to access, correct, delete or object to the use of personal data, or to withdraw consent, from members, guests, staff, caddies or applicants.

## Steps

1. **Receive.** Any channel (reception, app, Kijani Assist, email, letter). The receiving staff member forwards it to the Compliance Officer (DPO) the same day.
2. **Log.** The DPO records it in the [request log](data-subject-request-log.csv) with the date received and the due date (internal target: 7 days, within the statutory period).
3. **Verify identity.** Ask for proof proportionate to the request; do not ask for more data than needed.
4. **Search.** Check FairwayOne, Kijani Assist logs, CCTV (if within 30 days), HR files and email, using the [data inventory](../03-data-inventory/personal-data-inventory.csv).
5. **Respond.** Provide the data, make the correction or deletion, or explain any lawful reason for refusal and the right to complain to the ODPC.
6. **Close.** Record the response date and outcome. The DPO reports overdue requests to the General Manager weekly and the totals to the Finance and Audit sub-committee quarterly.
