# Project Charter: Enterprise Governance and Risk Dashboard

> **Portfolio project.** This is an independent learning project using a fictional organisation and illustrative data. It is not an audit, legal opinion, or certification.

Draft 0.1, prepared for review by Abel Holla.

## Purpose

The Stage 2 compliance review showed that new activities (the ANPR pilot, the Kijani Assist chatbot, cloud suppliers) were approved without committee oversight, and that decisions were minuted but not followed up. The Management Committee of Kijani Heights Golf and Country Club wants a governance framework that makes clear who decides what, how risk is overseen, and how decisions are tracked to closure.

## Scope

**Included**

- Governance framework: structure, principles, delegation of authority, reserved matters, committee terms of reference.
- Accountability: RACI matrix, escalation matrix, committee skills matrix, conflict-of-interest declarations.
- Risk governance: risk appetite statement, enterprise risk register, key risk indicators.
- Board oversight trail: a board paper, minutes, decision register and action register, with closure evidence.
- A governance maturity assessment against ISO 37000:2021 principles, with findings and corrective actions.
- A quarterly governance and risk dashboard and committee report.

**Excluded**

- Changing the Club's Articles or Bylaws (recommendations only).
- Legal advice on caddie employment status (flagged for a legal opinion).

## Intended outcomes

- Every significant decision has a clear decision-maker and an action that is tracked to closure.
- Risks are compared with the Club's appetite and escalated when they exceed it.
- The Management Committee sees compliance, risk and governance on one dashboard each quarter.

## Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Interest |
|---|---|
| Members | Confidence that the Club is well run |
| Management Committee | Clear authority and reliable oversight information |
| Finance and Audit sub-committee | Risk and compliance oversight |
| General Manager | Clear delegated authority |
| Compliance Officer (DPO) | Escalation route for compliance and data protection risk |
