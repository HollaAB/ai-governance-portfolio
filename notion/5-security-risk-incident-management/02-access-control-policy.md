## Principles

- **Least privilege.** People get only the access their role needs, as set in the [role access matrix](https://github.com/HollaAB/ai-governance-portfolio/blob/main/security-risk-incident-management/04-access-review/role-access-matrix.csv).
- **Individual accounts.** No shared logins. Every action can be traced to one person.
- **Multi-factor authentication.** Required for all administrator accounts, all remote access, and every account in FairwayOne, email and the payroll portal.
- **Segregation of duties.** No one can both create and approve a payment, or both sell and refund.

## Joiners, movers and leavers

| Event | Action | Who | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| Joiner | Request access by role on the access request form; manager approves | Line manager, ICT Officer | Before the first day |
| Mover | Remove old access, add new access | Line manager, ICT Officer | On the day of the move |
| Leaver | Disable all accounts, collect devices, keys and cards | HR Manager tells the ICT Officer | On the last working day |
| Supplier staff | Named accounts with an end date; reviewed quarterly | ICT Officer | At contract start and each quarter |

## Reviews

Owners of each system review all accounts every quarter against the role access matrix and HR leaver records. Privileged accounts are reviewed every month.
