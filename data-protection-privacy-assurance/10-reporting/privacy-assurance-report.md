# Privacy Assurance Report

> **Portfolio project.** This is an independent learning project using a fictional organisation and illustrative data. It is not an audit, legal opinion, or certification.

All results, dates and decisions are scenario results for the fictional Club.

**To:** Finance and Audit sub-committee and Management Committee, Kijani Heights Golf and Country Club
**From:** Compliance Officer (DPO)
**Period:** November to December 2026

## 1. Summary

The six data protection findings from the October compliance review are addressed: five are closed and one (retention of old records) is in progress. The Club now has a record of 14 processing activities, two approved DPIAs, data processing agreements with its four main processors, a retention schedule, a privacy notice, a request procedure and a breach plan that has already handled one minor incident. A follow-up review raised five smaller findings, mainly two missing agreements and staff training.
