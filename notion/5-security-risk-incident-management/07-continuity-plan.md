**Owner:** General Manager | **Reviewed by:** Security and Safety sub-committee

## Activation

The General Manager (or deputy) activates this plan when a critical process in the [business impact analysis](https://github.com/HollaAB/ai-governance-portfolio/blob/main/security-risk-incident-management/07-continuity/business-impact-analysis.csv) is down or likely to exceed its recovery time objective.

## Crisis team

General Manager (lead), ICT Officer, Head of Security, Finance Manager, Membership and Events Manager, Captain. The team meets in the Committee room or by phone.

## Scenarios and responses

| Scenario | Immediate response | Recovery |
|---|---|---|
| FairwayOne unavailable | Manual tee sheets; offline point of sale; tell members by SMS | FairwayOne restores; reconcile manual records within 24 hours |
| Point of sale down | Offline mode; paper chits | Re-sync when back; Finance checks totals |
| CCTV recorder fails | Extra guard patrols; note the gap in the security log | Replace or repair the recorder; spare unit needed (SF-10) |
| Power cut | Generator for clubhouse, gate and network | Check systems after power returns |
| Clubhouse unusable (fire, flood) | Evacuate; move reception to the pro shop | Insurers, repairs, temporary marquee for events |
| Key supplier fails | Use the exit clauses; temporary manual processes | Replace the supplier using the due diligence checklist |

## Testing

Recovery is tested once a year; results are in [recovery-test-results.csv](https://github.com/HollaAB/ai-governance-portfolio/blob/main/security-risk-incident-management/07-continuity/recovery-test-results.csv). The plan is reviewed after every activation or test.
