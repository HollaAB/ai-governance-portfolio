# Lessons Learned and Limitations

> **Portfolio project.** This is an independent learning project using a fictional organisation and illustrative data. It is not an audit, legal opinion, or certification.

## Lessons learned

1. **New technology carried most of the risk.** Tax, payroll and corporate filings were well controlled; the gaps were in cloud suppliers, AI and number-plate recognition, adopted without assessment.
2. **Many compliance gaps had a governance cause.** No rule said who approves high-risk technology, so nobody asked for a DPIA.
3. **Testing changed decisions.** The chatbot looked fine until 150 structured tests showed it leaked data; fixes were then measured, not assumed.
4. **Incidents expose the joins.** The exercise met the legal deadline but failed on a communication decision nobody owned.
5. **One register, many uses.** Keeping one obligations register and one risk register across projects made the evidence traceable end to end.
6. **The same weakness appears in several places.** Phishing awareness, shared accounts and supplier support each showed up in two or three projects; fixing them once is cheaper.

## Limitations

- **Fictional organisation and scenario results.** Every figure, decision and event is invented for the portfolio.
- **Legal references to verify.** Items marked "to verify" must be checked against official texts.
- **No technical testing.** No real systems were scanned or tested.
- **Frameworks as structure only.** ISO and NIST frameworks were used to organise the work; no certification or audit opinion is implied.
- **Snapshot.** Figures reflect the registers when this capstone was prepared (February 2027, scenario).
