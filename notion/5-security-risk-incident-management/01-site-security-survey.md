> **Portfolio project.** This is an independent learning project using a fictional organisation and illustrative data. It is not an audit, legal opinion, or certification.

All results, dates, accounts and events are scenario results for the fictional Club. No real systems, configurations or vulnerabilities are described.

Walked on 12 January 2027 by the Head of Security and the Compliance Officer. Descriptions are general; no layouts or weaknesses of any real site are shown.

## Zones

| Zone | Access | Controls seen | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main gate | Members, guests, suppliers | Guarded 24 hours, barrier, ANPR for opted-in members, visitor register, CCTV | Adequate; tailgating on busy days (SR-09) |
| Staff gate | Staff and deliveries | Guarded during the day, locked at night, CCTV | Adequate |
| Perimeter | None | Fence with gaps along the river boundary; night patrols | Partly adequate: two gaps reported to the Course Manager |
| Clubhouse | Members and guests | Reception by day, alarm at night, CCTV at entrances | Adequate |
| Cash office | Finance staff | Keyed door, safe, CCTV | Key register incomplete: 24 of 180 keys unaccounted for (SF-07) |
| Server and network cabinet | ICT Officer, ICT firm | Locked cabinet in the admin block | Adequate |
| Maintenance yard, fuel and chemical store | Course staff | Locked store, no camera covering the fuel tank, poor lighting | Not adequate (SF-06) |
| Staff car park | Staff | Unlit at the far end | Partly adequate (SF-06) |
