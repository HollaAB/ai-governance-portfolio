**Adopted by:** Management Committee, 28 October 2026 (decision D-2026-10-04, scenario)

## Purpose

To state how much risk the Club is willing to accept in pursuit of its purpose, so that managers know when to act on their own and when to escalate.

## Appetite levels

| Level | Meaning | Maximum risk rating accepted without escalation |
|---|---|---|
| Averse | Avoid the risk wherever possible | Low |
| Low | Accept only small, well-controlled risk | Medium (score up to 9) |
| Moderate | Accept some risk for clear benefit, with controls | High (score up to 12) |

Risk scores and ratings follow the scale in the [Stage 2 methodology](https://github.com/HollaAB/ai-governance-portfolio/blob/main/compliance-management-system/04-gap-assessment/assessment-methodology.md): 1 to 4 Low, 5 to 9 Medium, 10 to 16 High, 20 to 25 Critical.

## Appetite by category

| Category | Appetite | Tolerance |
|---|---|---|
| Health and safety | Averse | No serious injury or fatality; fire drills at least yearly |
| Legal and regulatory compliance | Low | No High compliance finding open beyond 30 days; no licence lapse |
| Data protection and privacy | Low | Every notifiable breach reported within 72 hours; no high-risk processing without a DPIA |
| Integrity and bribery | Averse | Zero tolerance; every concern investigated |
| Reputation with members | Low | No issue that leads to a members' petition or special general meeting |
| Financial sustainability | Moderate | Member arrears below 8% of annual subscriptions; reserves at least three months of costs |
| Technology and operations | Moderate | Club management system outages under 24 hours a year |
| Innovation in member services, including AI | Moderate | New services allowed after a DPIA and Committee approval where high-risk |
| Environment | Low | Water use within the permit; only registered pesticides |

## Escalation

A risk rated above the maximum for its category is **outside appetite**. Its owner escalates it under the [escalation matrix](https://github.com/HollaAB/ai-governance-portfolio/blob/main/enterprise-governance-risk-dashboard/04-accountability/escalation-matrix.csv). Only the Management Committee can accept a risk outside appetite, and it records that acceptance in the decision register with a review date.

## Review

The statement is reviewed every year and after any major incident.
