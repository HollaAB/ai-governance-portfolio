Refreshed terms for the two sub-committees that oversee risk, compliance and safety. The other sub-committees follow the same format.

## Finance and Audit sub-committee

**Purpose.** To oversee the Club's finances, internal control, risk management and compliance on behalf of the Management Committee.

**Membership.** The Honorary Treasurer (chair) and two other Committee members, at least one with finance or audit experience. The General Manager, Finance Manager and Compliance Officer attend but do not vote.

**Quorum.** Two members.

**Meetings.** At least quarterly, and when the chair calls one.

**Duties**

1. Review the quarterly accounts and the annual budget before they go to the Management Committee.
2. Review the quarterly compliance report, findings and overdue corrective actions.
3. Review the enterprise risk register and any risk outside appetite, and recommend acceptance or treatment.
4. Agree the annual compliance monitoring plan.
5. Meet the Compliance Officer without management present at least once a year.
6. Recommend the appointment of the external auditor.

**Reporting.** Minutes and recommendations go to the next Management Committee meeting.

## Security and Safety sub-committee

**Purpose.** To oversee the safety of members, guests and staff, and the security of Club property and information.

**Membership.** A Committee member (chair) and two other members, at least one with security or safety experience. The Head of Security, HR Manager and ICT Officer attend but do not vote.

**Quorum.** Two members.

**Meetings.** At least quarterly.

**Duties**

1. Review safety incidents, fire drills and audits, and the health and safety committee's minutes.
2. Review security incidents, access to CCTV, and the guarding contract's performance.
3. Review information security risks and the results of access reviews.
4. Oversee the business continuity and incident response plans.

**Reporting.** Minutes and recommendations go to the next Management Committee meeting.

## Review

Both terms of reference are reviewed every year by the Management Committee.
