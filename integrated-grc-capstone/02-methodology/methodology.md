# Methodology: Integrated GRC and Security Management System

Prepared by Abel Holla.

1. **Choose one case.** Use the Stage 5 "Saturday Medal" incident as the thread, because it touches every function.
2. **Trace requirements.** For each key requirement (law, contract or Club policy) the incident tested, follow the chain: requirement, control, test evidence, result, finding, corrective action, status. Record it in the [requirements-to-controls mapping](../04-requirements-mapping/requirements-to-controls-mapping.csv).
3. **Integrate risks.** Combine the enterprise risk register with the controls and open findings from all projects in the [integrated risk and control register](../04-requirements-mapping/integrated-risk-control-register.csv).
4. **Find root causes across functions.** Group project findings that share a cause into cross-cutting findings.
5. **Consolidate remediation.** Collect every open action from the five projects into one [roadmap](../05-findings-and-remediation/remediation-roadmap.csv), phased by due date.
6. **Report.** Build one dashboard, a Committee paper and an executive report.

## Reference frameworks

The integration follows the documented links between ISO 37301 (compliance), ISO 37000 (governance), ISO 31000 (risk), ISO/IEC 42001 and NIST AI RMF (AI), and NIST CSF 2.0 (security), as used in the earlier projects. No certification is implied.
