# Conflict of Interest Declaration Form

> **Portfolio project.** This is an independent learning project using a fictional organisation and illustrative data. It is not an audit, legal opinion, or certification.

To be completed by every Management Committee member and senior staff member on appointment, every January, and whenever an interest changes.

**Name:** ____________________ **Role:** ____________________ **Date:** ____________

1. Are you, or is a close family member, a director, owner, partner or employee of any business that supplies, or seeks to supply, the Club? If yes, give details.
2. Do you, or does a close family member, receive any payment or benefit from such a business?
3. Do you hold any other role that could conflict with your duties to the Club (for example in another club or a regulator)?
4. Have you received any gift or hospitality worth more than KES 10,000 from a person dealing with the Club in the last 12 months?

**Declaration.** I confirm the information is complete. I will declare any new interest at the start of the relevant meeting, leave the discussion and not vote.

**Signature:** ____________________
