## 1. Summary

The Committee's October decisions are being carried out. Two of the four High compliance findings are closed (breach procedure, fire drill); the other two are in progress and depend on the DPIAs and supplier contracts due in the data protection and AI projects. ## 3. Risks outside appetite

9 risks in the [enterprise risk register](https://github.com/HollaAB/ai-governance-portfolio/blob/main/enterprise-governance-risk-dashboard/05-risk-governance/enterprise-risk-register.csv) were outside appetite at the Q3 baseline:

- R-01: Personal data breach through cloud systems or supplier access (score 20, appetite Low)
- R-02: Unlawful processing by the ANPR pilot or Kijani Assist (score 16, appetite Low)
- R-03: Member data transferred abroad without safeguards (score 12, appetite Low)
- R-04: Licence or permit lapses (score 12, appetite Low)
- R-05: Bribery in procurement or member admission (score 10, appetite Averse)
- R-06: Fire or life-safety incident during a large event (score 10, appetite Averse)
- R-07: Food safety incident (score 8, appetite Averse)
- R-10: Account data disclosed through the chatbot (score 12, appetite Low)
- R-17: Conflicts of interest influence Committee decisions on suppliers (score 12, appetite Averse)

The breach procedure and the fire drill reduce R-01 and R-06, but no control has been retested yet, so all nine are re-scored after the retests and the Stage 4 and Stage 5 work. Two obligations (OB-05 breach notification and OB-20 fire drills) are now compliant, raising the compliant share from 50% to 56%. The Committee is asked to note that the remaining risks stay outside appetite until those actions close, and to record that decision.
