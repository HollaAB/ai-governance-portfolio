# AI Governance Report

> **Portfolio project.** This is an independent learning project using a fictional organisation and illustrative data. It is not an audit, legal opinion, or certification.

All results, dates and decisions are scenario results for the fictional Club.

**To:** Management Committee, Kijani Heights Golf and Country Club
**From:** AI review group
**Meeting:** 9 December 2026

## 1. Summary

The Club now knows every AI system it uses (7, including two proposals) and has classified them: 2 High, 1 Medium, 3 Low and 1 not permitted. The two High systems, Kijani Assist and gate ANPR, have been assessed, tested and fixed. The AI review group recommends approving Kijani Assist version 1.1 and ANPR in opt-in mode.
