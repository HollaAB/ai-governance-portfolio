# Executive Summary: Integrated GRC and Security Management System

> **Portfolio project.** This is an independent learning project using a fictional organisation and illustrative data. It is not an audit, legal opinion, or certification.

All results, dates and decisions are scenario results for the fictional Club.

**The problem.** A members' golf club's compliance, governance, data, AI and security work had been done project by project. A single phishing incident crosses all of them.

**What was done.** The five projects were joined around one incident exercise. 16 key requirements were traced from law or policy to control, test, finding and action. Risks from all projects were combined against the Club's appetite, shared root causes were grouped into five cross-cutting findings, and every open action went into one roadmap with a dashboard and a Committee paper.

**What was found.** Obligations compliant rose from 18 to 25 of 36, and every High compliance finding is closed. The ODPC deadline can be met, but member warnings, supplier support in incidents, account security, staff awareness and recovery still fall short. 6 of 18 enterprise risks remain outside appetite.

**What is recommended.** Approve the roadmap of 28 actions, delegate fast member warnings to the DPO, strengthen critical supplier contracts, and fund combined training and continuity fixes.

**What this demonstrates.** Connecting compliance, governance and security in one business scenario, with traceable evidence and a single plan a board can act on.
