# Compliance Monitoring Plan, 2027

> **Portfolio project.** This is an independent learning project using a fictional organisation and illustrative data. It is not an audit, legal opinion, or certification.

Draft 0.2, prepared for review by Abel Holla.

Controls are tested on a cycle set by risk. Results go into the testing workpapers and the quarterly report.

| Quarter | Controls tested | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 (January to March) | CTL-05 breach response, CTL-06 DPIA, CTL-07 processor contracts, CTL-16 licence register | High findings in 2026 (F-01, F-02, F-03, F-13); licence renewals due |
| Q2 (April to June) | CTL-02 privacy notices, CTL-04 data subject requests, CTL-09 retention, CTL-13 safety file | Medium findings; fire drill follow-up (F-11) |
| Q3 (July to September) | CTL-10 anti-bribery, CTL-11 personnel files, CTL-17 food safety, CTL-18 environmental file | Medium and low findings; annual audits due |
| Q4 (October to December) | All remaining controls, annual applicability review, CTL-22 regulatory change | Full annual cycle |

## Monthly checks

- Statutory filings (CTL-15): confirm each filing and payment by its deadline.
- Data subject request log (CTL-04): confirm no request is overdue.
- Corrective-action tracker: confirm no action is overdue.

## Reporting

The Compliance Officer reports results, open findings and overdue actions to the Finance and Audit sub-committee each quarter, using the KPIs in [kpi-definitions.csv](../07-reporting/kpi-definitions.csv).
