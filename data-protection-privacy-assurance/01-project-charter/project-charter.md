# Project Charter: Data Protection and Privacy Assurance

> **Portfolio project.** This is an independent learning project using a fictional organisation and illustrative data. It is not an audit, legal opinion, or certification.

Prepared by Abel Holla.

## Purpose

The Stage 2 compliance review found the Club's biggest gaps in data protection: no record of processing, no DPIAs for the ANPR pilot or the Kijani Assist chatbot, processor contracts without data protection terms, no retention schedule, and no breach procedure. This project builds the Club's data protection programme and closes those findings.

## Scope

**Included:** personal data inventory (record of processing activities); data-flow map; DPIAs for gate CCTV with ANPR and for Kijani Assist; processor register and cross-border transfer assessment; retention schedule; privacy notice and CCTV sign text; data subject request procedure and log; breach response plan and breach log; a privacy assurance review with findings and corrective actions; reporting.

**Excluded:** the AI-specific governance of Kijani Assist (covered in the [AI Governance Framework](../../ai-governance-framework/README.md)), and legal advice.

## Intended outcomes

- Every use of personal data is recorded with its purpose, lawful basis, systems, recipients and retention.
- High-risk processing is assessed before it runs, with measures the Committee has approved.
- Every processor is under a written agreement, and transfers outside Kenya have safeguards.
- Members and visitors are told how their data is used, and their requests are answered on time.
- A breach can be assessed and, where required, notified to the ODPC within 72 hours.
