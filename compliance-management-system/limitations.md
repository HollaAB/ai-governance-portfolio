# Limitations: Compliance Management System

> **Portfolio project.** This is an independent learning project using a fictional organisation and illustrative data. It is not an audit, legal opinion, or certification.

- **Fictional organisation.** The Club, its people, suppliers and figures are invented. Results are scenario results, not findings about any real club.
- **Legal references.** The register's legal references were checked against the official texts by Abel Holla (October 2026). Laws change; the regulatory change-monitoring procedure keeps the register current.
- **Not legal advice.** The register shows how obligations can be organised and tracked. It does not interpret the law for any real organisation.
- **Scenario testing.** The Stage 2 test results, samples and exceptions are invented for the fictional Club to show how a gap assessment works. They are not observations of any real club.
- **Sampling.** Samples follow the sampling table in the methodology; a real assessment might need larger samples for some populations.
- **Not complete.** A real club would also review its leases, insurance policies, all supplier contracts and any sector rules for activities it adds later.
