# Compliance Management Report

> **Portfolio project.** This is an independent learning project using a fictional organisation and illustrative data. It is not an audit, legal opinion, or certification.

Prepared by Abel Holla.

**To:** Finance and Audit sub-committee, Kijani Heights Golf and Country Club
**From:** Compliance Officer (DPO)
**Period:** gap assessment, 12 to 23 October 2026 (scenario dates)

All results are scenario results for the fictional Club.

## 1. Summary

The Club now has a register of 36 applicable obligations, each with an owner, a control and evidence. The first gap assessment found the Club **compliant with 18 (50%)**, **partly compliant with 14** and **not compliant with 4** obligations. Tax, payroll, corporate filings and most licences are well controlled. The main gaps are in data protection: no breach procedure, no DPIAs for the ANPR pilot and the Kijani Assist chatbot, and processor contracts without data protection terms.

## 4. Key risks

The [risk and control register](../risk-and-control-register.csv) rates the risk of a personal data breach (R-01) and unlawful processing by the ANPR pilot or chatbot (R-02) as the highest. Both depend on the actions for F-01 to F-03. The risk that caddies could be treated as employees (R-09) has no control yet and is noted for the governance review in Stage 3.
