> **Portfolio project.** This is an independent learning project using a fictional organisation and illustrative data. It is not an audit, legal opinion, or certification.

Draft 0.1, prepared for review by Abel Holla.

Each AI use case is scored on six criteria (0 = no, 1 = partly, 2 = yes). The total sets the tier, but any criterion marked "always high" makes the system High regardless of the total.

| # | Criterion | Always high? |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | Affects a person's access, money, employment or rights | Yes, if the AI decides without human review |
| C2 | Uses personal data, especially sensitive data | |
| C3 | Acts on its own (makes or carries out decisions) rather than informing a person | |
| C4 | Faces members or the public directly | |
| C5 | Generative output that could be wrong or misleading | |
| C6 | Vendor model the Club cannot inspect | |

| Total | Tier | Approval |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 3 | Low | System owner |
| 4 to 6 | Medium | AI review group |
| 7 to 12 | High | Management Committee, after impact assessment, DPIA and testing |

A use case is **not permitted** if it would make significant decisions about people with no practical human review, or if its risks cannot be reduced to an acceptable level. Results: [use-case-classification.csv](https://github.com/HollaAB/ai-governance-portfolio/blob/main/ai-governance-framework/04-classification/use-case-classification.csv).
