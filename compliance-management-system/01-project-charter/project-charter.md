# Project Charter: Compliance Management System

> **Portfolio project.** This is an independent learning project using a fictional organisation and illustrative data. It is not an audit, legal opinion, or certification.

Draft 0.1, prepared for review by Abel Holla.

## Purpose

The Management Committee of Kijani Heights Golf and Country Club wants one reliable view of what the Club must comply with, who is responsible, and how compliance is shown. This project builds that compliance management system for the fictional Club described in the [organisation profile](../../scenario/organisation-profile.md).

## Scope

**Included**

- Stage 1: identify the Club's legal, regulatory, contractual and internal obligations; assess which apply; assign owners; map them to controls; set a compliance calendar; list the evidence for each.
- Stage 2: test whether the controls work (gap assessment), record findings, and plan corrective actions.

**Excluded**

- Legal advice. The register records obligations for planning purposes and does not interpret the law for any real organisation.
- Sector rules that do not apply to a members' golf club.

## Intended outcomes

- An obligations register covering data protection, anti-bribery, employment, health and safety, tax, licensing, environment, corporate and contractual duties.
- A proposed control for every applicable obligation.
- A compliance calendar so no renewal or filing is missed.
- An evidence register showing where proof of compliance is kept.
- A tested view of compliance gaps and a corrective-action plan (Stage 2).

## Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Interest |
|---|---|
| Management Committee | Oversight and assurance that the Club is compliant |
| Finance and Audit sub-committee | Reviews compliance reports each quarter |
| General Manager | Accountable for compliance across operations |
| Compliance Officer (DPO) | Runs the system day to day |
| Department heads | Own individual obligations and controls |
