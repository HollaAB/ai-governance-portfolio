# Limitations: AI Governance Framework

> **Portfolio project.** This is an independent learning project using a fictional organisation and illustrative data. It is not an audit, legal opinion, or certification.

- **Fictional organisation.** The Club, Sauti AI Labs, ModelCo, test results and incidents are invented.
- **Scenario testing.** The 150-prompt test set is described, not published, and results are scenario results. A real test set would be larger and refreshed regularly.
- **Framework use.** NIST AI RMF and ISO/IEC 42001 are used as structure only; no certification is implied.
- **Legal context to verify.** References to Kenya's National AI Strategy and to s.35 of the Data Protection Act must be checked against official texts.
- **No technical testing of the model.** Testing covers behaviour through the chatbot, not the vendor's model internals.
