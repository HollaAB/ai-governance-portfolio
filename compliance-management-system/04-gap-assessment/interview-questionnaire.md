# Interview Questionnaire

> **Portfolio project.** This is an independent learning project using a fictional organisation and illustrative data. It is not an audit, legal opinion, or certification.

Used with each obligation owner during fieldwork. Notes are kept in the testing workpapers.

## For every owner

1. Which obligations in the register are you responsible for? Do you agree with that list?
2. How do you make sure each one is met? Who does the work day to day?
3. What records show it was done, and where are they kept?
4. When was it last done? Was it on time?
5. Has anything gone wrong in the last 12 months (late filing, complaint, inspection finding, penalty)?
6. What would stop you meeting the obligation (staff, budget, systems, suppliers)?
7. How would you find out if the law or the contract changed?

## Data protection (Compliance Officer, ICT Officer, Membership and Events Manager)

8. Which systems hold personal data, and who can access them?
9. Which suppliers process personal data for the Club? Do contracts cover data protection?
10. What happens if a laptop is lost or a system is hacked? Who is told, and when?
11. How are requests from members to see or delete their data handled?
12. Was any assessment done before the ANPR pilot or the Kijani Assist chatbot started?
13. How long are CCTV footage and former members' records kept?

## Anti-bribery (General Manager, Finance Manager)

14. How are gifts and hospitality from suppliers recorded?
15. How can staff or members report a concern in confidence?

## Health, safety and licensing (HR Manager, Head of Security, F&B Manager, Course Manager)

16. When were the last fire drill and fire audit?
17. How are licence and certificate expiry dates tracked?
18. How are food handlers' medical certificates kept current?
19. Which pesticides are used, and how is their registration checked?
