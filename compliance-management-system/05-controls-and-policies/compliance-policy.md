# Compliance Policy

> **Portfolio project.** This is an independent learning project using a fictional organisation and illustrative data. It is not an audit, legal opinion, or certification.

Prepared by Abel Holla.

**Approved by:** Management Committee (pending) | **Owner:** Compliance Officer (DPO) | **Review:** every year

## 1. Purpose

Kijani Heights Golf and Country Club will meet its legal, regulatory, contractual and internal obligations, and will be able to show that it does.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to the Management Committee, all employees, caddies registered with the Club, and suppliers acting for the Club.

## 3. Principles

- The Club keeps one register of its obligations, each with an owner, a control and evidence.
- Owners meet their obligations on time and keep the evidence.
- Controls are tested at least once a year, high-risk ones more often.
- Gaps are reported, not hidden. Each gap gets a corrective action with an owner and a deadline.
- New activities, systems and suppliers are checked for compliance before they start.

## 4. Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Management Committee | Approves this policy and oversees compliance |
| Finance and Audit sub-committee | Receives the quarterly compliance report and tracks corrective actions |
| General Manager | Accountable for compliance across operations |
| Compliance Officer (DPO) | Maintains the register, calendar and monitoring plan; reports quarterly |
| Department heads | Own their obligations and controls; keep evidence |
| All staff | Follow Club procedures and report concerns |

## 5. Reporting concerns

Anyone can report a compliance concern to their manager, the Compliance Officer, or the confidential reporting channel. The Club does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith.

## 6. Breaches of this policy

Breaches are investigated and may lead to disciplinary action under the Club's HR procedures.
