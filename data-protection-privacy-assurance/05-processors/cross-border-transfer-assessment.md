# Cross-Border Transfer Assessment

> **Portfolio project.** This is an independent learning project using a fictional organisation and illustrative data. It is not an audit, legal opinion, or certification.

All results, dates and decisions are scenario results for the fictional Club.

The Data Protection Act 2019 (s.48 and s.49) allows transfers of personal data outside Kenya only with appropriate safeguards. This assessment covers the Club's two transfers.

## Transfer 1: member records to FairwayOne (South Africa)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What data | All member records, bookings and billing (PA-01 to PA-03, PA-05) |
| Why abroad | FairwayOne hosts its service in South Africa; no Kenyan hosting option |
| Destination law | South Africa has a data protection law (POPIA) and a regulator |
| Safeguards | Data processing agreement signed 10 December 2026: processing only on the Club's instructions, security measures, breach notice within 24 hours, sub-processors only with approval, return and deletion at exit, audit rights |
| Security evidence | FairwayOne's latest independent security report reviewed by the ICT Officer |
| Conclusion | Transfer may continue under the agreement. Review yearly. |

## Transfer 2: chatbot question text to ModelCo (outside Kenya)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What data | Text of members' questions to Kijani Assist (PA-04). No account data. |
| Why abroad | The language model is only offered from outside Kenya |
| Safeguards | Sauti AI Labs' agreement with the Club (20 November 2026) binds ModelCo as a sub-processor: no training on Club data, deletion within 30 days, security measures |
| Residual concern | The Club relies on Sauti AI Labs for ModelCo's compliance; ModelCo's certifications are not independently verified (AI finding AIF-03) |
| Conclusion | Transfer may continue; warn members not to share sensitive information in chat; review in six months. |

## Notes

The transfers rely on the safeguards in s.48 and s.49. Legal references were checked against the official texts by Abel Holla (October 2026).
