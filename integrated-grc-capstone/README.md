# Integrated GRC and Security Management System

> **Portfolio project.** This is an independent learning project using a fictional organisation and illustrative data. It is not an audit, legal opinion, or certification.

**Status:** Draft complete, for review | **Stage:** 6 | **Area:** Compliance; Governance; Security; AI & Data Governance

**Organisation:** Kijani Heights Golf and Country Club (fictional), see the [organisation profile](../scenario/organisation-profile.md)

**Notion page:** added after the site is published

## 1. Overview and business problem

The five earlier projects each covered one function. The "Saturday Medal" phishing exercise showed that a single incident crosses security, compliance and governance at once. This capstone joins them into one evidence trail and one plan.

## 2. Objectives, scope and assumptions

Trace key requirements end to end, combine risks, find shared root causes, and consolidate every open action into one roadmap for the Management Committee. No new testing. Assumptions: [assumptions register](01-project-charter/assumptions-register.csv).

## 3. Frameworks and methodology

Links the frameworks used in the earlier projects (ISO 37301, ISO 37000, ISO 31000, ISO/IEC 42001, NIST AI RMF, NIST CSF 2.0). No certification is claimed. Details: [methodology](02-methodology/methodology.md).

## 5. Testing results or evaluation criteria

Scenario results: 16 requirements traced, 9 met; 25 of 36 obligations compliant (from 18); 6 of 18 risks outside appetite; in the exercise the ODPC deadline was met and the member warning was not.

## 6. Recommendations and implementation roadmap

Approve the roadmap of 28 open actions in three phases; delegate fast member warnings to the DPO; add incident support terms to critical supplier contracts; fund combined training and continuity fixes. See the [committee paper](06-dashboard/committee-paper.md) and [executive report](07-executive/executive-report.md).
