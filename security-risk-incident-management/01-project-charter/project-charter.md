# Project Charter: Security Risk and Incident Management

> **Portfolio project.** This is an independent learning project using a fictional organisation and illustrative data. It is not an audit, legal opinion, or certification.

Draft 0.1, prepared for review by Abel Holla.

## Purpose

The Club's data protection and AI work (Stage 4) depends on systems and people being secure. The highest risk in the [enterprise risk register](../../enterprise-governance-risk-dashboard/05-risk-governance/enterprise-risk-register.csv) is still a personal data breach through cloud systems or supplier access (R-01), and the Club has never tested how it would respond to an incident or recover from an outage. This project assesses security risk across the Club's sites and systems, reviews who has access to what, builds an incident response capability and tests it.

## Scope

**Included:** asset inventory; threat and vulnerability assessment; security risk register; site security survey; access-control policy, role access matrix and user access review; phishing simulation; incident response plan, severity matrix, playbooks and chain-of-custody form; a tabletop exercise with a full incident chronology; business impact analysis, continuity and recovery plan, and recovery tests; findings, corrective actions and reporting.

**Excluded:** technical penetration testing or scanning (not authorised and not needed for this portfolio); real system configurations.

## Intended outcomes

- The Club knows its critical assets and the risks to them.
- Only the right people have access, and leavers lose it the same day.
- Staff know what to do in an incident, and the 72-hour breach clock is met.
- Critical services can continue or recover within agreed times.
